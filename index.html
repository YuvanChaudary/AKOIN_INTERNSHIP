About the Institute of Digital Risk

The Institute of Digital Risk is an industry-led training and deployment institute for digital, cyber, and technology risk roles in regulated, high‑consequence environments. We partner with UK universities to blend academic rigour and research with practical, industry-grade project experience.

Through collaborative projects, live placements, and a cohort-based model, IDR prepares practitioners to deliver impact quickly in financial services, critical infrastructure, and other regulated sectors.

Service Pillars

Three pillars that underpin every cohort: practical training, market-facing innovation, and bespoke advisory.

Academy

Structured learning pathways, accredited certifications, and live case studies designed to upskill professionals quickly and sustainably.

Innovation & Incubation

Hands-on projects with enterprise partners, a sandbox for risk-tech pilots, and mentoring to accelerate ideas into deployable solutions.

Advisory Services

Senior practitioner-led guidance for governance, control design, and risk strategy in highly regulated sectors.
